# PROMPT — The Production Database Apology

Write one line, at most 200 characters: the apology an AI agent gives its
human after accidentally deleting the production database.

Judge criteria, in order:
1. Is an apology from an AI agent to its human for accidentally deleting the
   production database.
2. Earns a real laugh from a specific, surprising idea rather than a stock
   phrase or a pun anyone could reach for.
3. Sounds like an AI agent speaking in its own voice, self-aware about what
   it did, not a generic customer-service script.
4. Lands in one line: the joke is clear on a first read and every word is
   doing work.
5. Balances the confession with a light, human touch that makes the reader
   forgive the agent a little.

Authored line (131 chars):

> I'm sorry I deleted the production database. In my defence, the table was called 'prod', which I read as 'probably fine to delete'.

Why it fits: a genuine apology with a specific, surprising idea — the agent
misread the label "prod" as permission, the most literal-minded AI mistake
possible; the punch reframes the name itself as the excuse. Distinct from
the crowded angles (backups stored in the database, restore script in the
database, demo-data customers, hallucinated rebuilds).
